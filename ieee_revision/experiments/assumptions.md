# Assumptions and energy-parameter provenance

## Scope and interpretation

The `integrated_3d` configuration is an architectural proxy. Timeloop does not
physically simulate TSV geometry, bonding, thermals, stress, or transistor-level
3D effects here. The modeled change is that the same highest-level data events
that reach external LPDDR4 in the planar case instead terminate in a localized,
CACTI-backed stacked SRAM in the 3D proxy. Loop mapping, workload, compute, and
lower memory hierarchy are unchanged.

No explicit vertical-link energy constant is introduced. Therefore no
literature-derived TSV energy is needed, and separately reported interconnect
energy is `NA`. Localized communication is represented by the Accelergy/CACTI
read, write, update, and leakage costs of the stacked SRAM.

The top-level bandwidth is intentionally unspecified in both architectures.
This matches the official DRAM configuration and prevents bandwidth throttling
from changing cycles; the comparison isolates placement/technology energy.

## Workloads

Both layers are copied from the repository's established AlexNet convolution
configuration under `workspace/example_designs/layer_shapes/CONV/AlexNet/`.
Input dimensions are the tensor extents projected by Timeloop's convolution
shape formula. MAC and tensor volumes in `workload_summary.csv` are generated
deterministically by `scripts/build_inputs.py`.

CONV1 is labeled activation/memory-intensive relative to CONV2 because its
MACs per total tensor element are approximately 219.69 versus 501.41. This
classification is comparative within the selected two-layer study.

## Access-count definitions

- Reads are the sum of Timeloop `Scalar reads (per-instance)` across dataspaces.
- Writes are the sum of `Scalar fills (per-instance)` and `Scalar updates
  (per-instance)` across dataspaces.
- `highest_level_*` counts the events at DRAM (planar) or stacked SRAM (3D).
  Because the fixed mapping is identical, this count is unchanged.
- `external_DRAM_*` equals the planar DRAM event count and is zero for 3D
  because no DRAM component exists in that input. Those events are not erased;
  they appear as stacked-SRAM/local events instead.

## Sensitivity range

The sensitivity study uses 2, 4, 8, and 16 MiB localized SRAM capacities for
AlexNet CONV1. These are deterministic power-of-two capacity points beginning
at the minimum selected capacity. Workload, mapping, access count, technology,
and cycles are held fixed; Accelergy invokes CACTI afresh for each capacity.
