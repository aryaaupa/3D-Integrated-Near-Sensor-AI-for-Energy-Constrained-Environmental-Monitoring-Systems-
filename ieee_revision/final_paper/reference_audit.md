## CACTI DOI disposition

The CACTI 6.0 paper is identified by title, authors, venue, pages, year, and IEEE Xplore document 4408241. The manuscript intentionally omits a DOI because publisher metadata conflict: IEEE pages associate document 4408241 with `10.1109/MICRO.2007.33`, while ACM indexing associates the same title with `.30` and assigns `.33` elsewhere. This audit does not resolve that inconsistency by guesswork.
