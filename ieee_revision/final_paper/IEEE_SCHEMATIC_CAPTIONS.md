# IEEE schematic captions and placement

The three schematics are generated by `scripts/generate_all_figures.py` as PDF,
SVG, and 600-dpi PNG files. They contain no performance, thermal, accuracy,
bandwidth, floorplan, geometry, or interconnect-count claims.

## Fig. 1 - Three-tier architecture

**Caption:** Fig. 1. Proposed three-tier near-sensor organization. The sensor,
localized-SRAM, and compute tiers are shown as a logical stack with conceptual
vertical inter-tier communication. The compute tier depicts the evaluated
168-PE array and 128-KiB global buffer. The schematic does not specify physical
tier dimensions, bonding pitch, interconnect count, floorplan, or thermal
boundaries.

**Placement and supported claim:** Insert in Section III-A after the paragraph
introducing the three logical tiers. It supports only the proposed architectural
topology and identifies the two evaluated compute-tier structures.

Files: `figures/fig1_architecture.pdf`, `.svg`, and `.png`.

## Fig. 2 - Two-level evaluation workflow

**Caption:** Fig. 2. Independent architecture- and circuit-level evaluation
paths. Timeloop and Accelergy/CACTI produce architecture-level energy estimates;
the PTM/ngspice link testbench produces switching-energy and propagation-delay
estimates. The paths meet only at separate cross-level interpretation and are
not co-simulated.

**Placement and supported claim:** Insert in Section III-B or at the opening of
Section IV. It supports the methodological separation of the architecture and
circuit studies without repeating qualifications already stated in the text.

Files: `figures/fig2_evaluation_workflow.pdf`, `.svg`, and `.png`.

## Fig. 3 - Controlled hierarchy comparison

**Caption:** Fig. 3. Controlled planar and localized-memory hierarchies. The
highest-level component is external LPDDR4 in the planar baseline and a 2-MiB
localized SRAM in the controlled variant. The mapping, arithmetic precision,
clock, 128-KiB SRAM, PE register files, and 168-PE array are identical.

**Placement and supported claim:** Insert in Section III-C beside the
fair-comparison description. It supports the claim that only the highest-level
memory placement/type changes between paired evaluations.

Files: `figures/fig3_controlled_hierarchy.pdf`, `.svg`, and `.png`.
